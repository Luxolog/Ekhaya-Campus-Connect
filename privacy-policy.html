CampusConnect Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

Launch requirement: This notice is an implementation draft. Before live collection, the institution using CampusConnect must confirm its legal identity and privacy contact, and the platform operator must complete the processor, hosting, retention and cross-border transfer details. Do not treat this draft as legal certification.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

For student applications and institutional services, the college or institution that determines why and how your information is used is generally the responsible party under POPIA. Ekhaya Technologies provides and operates the CampusConnect platform and may act as an operator processing information on that institution's documented instructions. The responsible institution's name and privacy contact must be shown on the relevant application before that application goes live.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the feature you use, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, province, town, education history, account credentials (handled by the authentication provider), application details and information you choose to submit. Do not submit identity documents or sensitive information unless the relevant institution specifically requests it through an approved secure process.

3. Why information is used

Purposes may include creating and securing your account, maintaining your student profile, responding to enquiries, administering applications you choose to make, communicating application updates and meeting legal obligations. Information is not shared with an institution for an application unless the relevant application flow explains the sharing and you choose to submit it.

4. Lawful basis and choices

Each responsible institution must identify and document an applicable POPIA lawful justification for each purpose. Acknowledging this notice confirms that you have been informed; it is not a blanket consent to every future use. Optional marketing must be a separate, unticked choice and may be withdrawn at any time.

5. Service providers and transfers

CampusConnect currently uses Supabase authentication and database services for account-related features. Before production, the operator and responsible institution must document all service providers, their access, security safeguards, processing locations and any cross-border transfers, and put appropriate operator agreements and transfer safeguards in place.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards must protect information against loss, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration. Information must be retained only for as long as lawfully required for the stated purpose, then securely deleted or de-identified. The institution must publish its applicable retention periods before collecting application records.

7. Your POPIA rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, request deletion where applicable, and complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. Use the privacy contact published by the responsible institution. You may also visit the Information Regulator.

8. Children

If a feature is intended for children or collects information from a child, the responsible party must assess POPIA's special rules for children's information and obtain prior authorisation from a competent person where required before collection.

9. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the platform and institution's processing change. The responsible institution's name, Information Officer details and privacy request channel must be supplied here and on each live application before production launch.
